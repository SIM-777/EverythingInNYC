Best Pizza in NYC for Tourists: Where to Go, What to Order, and What Is Worth the Trip

17 NYC pizzerias compared by neighborhood, style, wait, and value — matched to your itinerary so you spend your time eating pizza, not chasing it

New York does not have one single "best pizza." The right place depends on the kind of pizza you want, where you are sightseeing, how much time you have, and whether you want a quick slice or a sit-down meal.

For a classic New York slice, start with Joe's. For a more modern slice with creative toppings, choose L'Industrie. For a whole pie in a historic setting, try John's of Bleecker Street. If you are visiting the Brooklyn Bridge, L&B Spumoni Gardens in DUMBO is the most convenient destination for a distinctive Sicilian square.

The most important advice is simple: do not spend your entire vacation crossing the city for famous pizza. Several of the best restaurants fit naturally into sightseeing days, while others are only worth visiting if pizza is one of your main reasons for coming to New York.

NYC Pizza Styles Explained

Classic New York Slice

This is the large, thin, foldable slice most visitors picture when they think of New York pizza. It is usually sold by the slice at a counter and eaten quickly. Try Joe's, Best Pizza, Scarr's, or NY Pizza Suprema.

Neapolitan Pizza

Neapolitan pizza comes as a smaller whole pie with a soft, blistered crust. It is a sit-down meal rather than a grab-and-go slice. Try Song' E Napule or Una Pizza Napoletana.

Sicilian and Grandma Pizza

These are square pizzas. Sicilian slices are thicker and airier, while grandma-style pizza is generally thinner and crisper. L&B Spumoni Gardens is the clearest Sicilian choice. Mama's TOO!, Best Pizza, Fini, and Scarr's also offer substantial square slices.

Modern and Specialty Pizza

Modern NYC pizzerias use the city's slice tradition as a base for toppings such as burrata, hot honey, fig jam, vodka sauce, or Cacio e Pepe. Try L'Industrie, Mama's TOO!, Rubirosa, or Fini.

Detroit-Style-Influenced Pizza

Some NYC pizzerias use thick, crisp-edged dough and caramelized cheese while still serving New York-style toppings. Mama's TOO! is the most useful choice in this guide if you want that texture.

Best Pizza Near Times Square and Penn Station

For Broadway or Times Square, choose Joe's Pizza at 1435 Broadway. It is the most direct classic-slice option. For Madison Square Garden, Penn Station, or a train connection, choose NY Pizza Suprema at 413 8th Avenue. It serves classic New York slices and whole pies, offers pickup and delivery, and is currently listed as open daily from approximately 10:30 AM–11 PM.

Convenience versus quality

These are convenience recommendations rather than reasons to cross Manhattan. If you are already downtown, Joe's Carmine Street, L'Industrie, Scarr's, or Rubirosa are stronger reasons to plan a pizza stop.

Best Pizza by Neighborhood

Greenwich Village and the West Village

Choose Joe's, John's, Song' E Napule, or the West Village L'Industrie location. A sensible plan is one slice at Joe's followed by either a whole pie at John's or a Neapolitan dinner at Song' E Napule.

SoHo, Nolita, and Little Italy

Choose L'Industrie Little Italy, Rubirosa, or Scarr's. These restaurants work well with shopping, Chinatown, the Tenement Museum, Ferrara Bakery, and the New Museum. Full food day planning for this area: How to Build a NYC Food Day Without Reservations →

Lower East Side and East Village

Choose Scarr's, Una Pizza Napoletana, or Artichoke. Scarr's is the most flexible. Una requires advance planning. Artichoke is the late-night option.

Upper West Side and Central Park

Choose Mama's TOO! for creative squares or Koronet for a giant late-night slice. Mama's TOO! is better for flavor and unusual toppings. Koronet is better when size and convenience matter most.

Williamsburg

Choose Best Pizza, Fini, or L'Industrie. These restaurants can be combined into a neighborhood food outing, but two stops are generally enough.

Bushwick

Choose Ops for a sit-down pizza-and-wine meal. Do not treat Bushwick as a quick extension of a Williamsburg crawl. It requires its own evening.

DUMBO and Brooklyn Heights

Choose L&B Spumoni Gardens DUMBO. It is the most practical pizza choice after the Brooklyn Bridge or during a Brooklyn waterfront day. For the full Brooklyn day plan: One Day in Brooklyn: DUMBO, Brooklyn Bridge & Beyond →

Midwood

Choose Di Fara only if the pizza pilgrimage itself is important to you.

Jersey City

Choose Razza if you are willing to cross the Hudson for a destination meal.

A Planned Neapolitan Dinner

Choose either Song' E Napule or Una Pizza Napoletana. Song' E Napule is easier to combine with Greenwich Village sightseeing and has broader restaurant service. Una is more specialized and operates only Thursday–Saturday, so reserve it before arranging the rest of your evening.

A Family-Friendly Plan

Choose L&B DUMBO, Rubirosa, Song' E Napule, or John's. These options provide seating and are easier for groups than standing-only slice shops. Famous Pizza Places That Are Not Right for Everyone

Prince Street Pizza

The spicy pepperoni square remains a distinctive order, but it is not worth crossing the city for if the line is long. Go when you are already in Nolita and the wait is short.

Lucali

A full-evening commitment. The no-reservation system, cash-only policy, and potential multi-hour wait make it unsuitable for many tourists.

Di Fara

Makes sense for a dedicated pizza-history trip, not for a casual addition to a Manhattan sightseeing day. Current experiences are too divided to treat it as an automatic must-visit.

Joe's Times Square

Convenient and recognizable, but the Carmine Street location is the better choice when you can reach Greenwich Village.

Grimaldi's and Juliana's (DUMBO)

Both are useful to know about in DUMBO, but their famous coal-oven pies can involve long waits. L&B gives you a different Brooklyn style in the same sightseeing area and is usually the more efficient plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best pizza in NYC for first-time visitors?▾
Start with Joe's for a classic slice. Add John's if you want a historic whole pie or L'Industrie if you prefer creative toppings.
Where can tourists find the best New York slice?▾
Joe's, Scarr's, Best Pizza, and NY Pizza Suprema are the most useful choices. Joe's is the clearest introduction to the classic foldable slice.
What is the best pizza near Times Square?▾
Joe's Pizza at 1435 Broadway is the most convenient classic-slice option. NY Pizza Suprema is more useful around Penn Station and Madison Square Garden.
Is it worth traveling to Brooklyn for pizza?▾
Yes, if you are already visiting DUMBO or Williamsburg. L&B DUMBO, Best Pizza, Fini, and L'Industrie can fit naturally into Brooklyn sightseeing. Di Fara and Lucali require more commitment.
Which NYC pizzerias serve slices?▾
Joe's, L'Industrie, Mama's TOO!, Scarr's, L&B Spumoni Gardens, Best Pizza, Fini, Di Fara, Artichoke, Koronet, and NY Pizza Suprema serve slices.
Which places require reservations?▾
Reservations are especially useful or necessary at Rubirosa, Song' E Napule, Una Pizza Napoletana, Ops, and Razza. Lucali does not take reservations and uses a waitlist.
What is the best pizza for families?▾
Choose L&B DUMBO, Rubirosa, Song' E Napule, John's, or Razza because they offer seated meals. Standing-only shops are less comfortable for families with children.
What is the best late-night pizza in NYC?▾
Joe's is best for a classic late-night slice. Artichoke is best for a rich specialty slice, while Koronet is best for an enormous portion.
How much does NYC pizza cost?▾
A standard slice generally costs about $4–$8. Specialty slices can cost up to approximately $10. Whole pies range from roughly $20 to $50, depending on the restaurant and toppings.
Which famous pizza places are overrated or inconvenient?▾
Prince Street is conditional when the line is long. Lucali is difficult because of its wait and cash-only policy. Di Fara is far from the main tourist areas and has mixed current experiences.
Where should I go if I only have time for one pizza stop?▾
Choose the restaurant nearest your itinerary. Joe's is the best all-purpose answer, L&B DUMBO is best near the Brooklyn Bridge, Mama's TOO! is best near Central Park and the Upper West Side, and NY Pizza Suprema is best near Penn Station.
